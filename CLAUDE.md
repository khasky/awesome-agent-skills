# CLAUDE.md

`AGENTS.md` in this repository is the source; this file mirrors its two standing rules so they are in reach either way.

- Examples in a skill are abstract. No real post, account, person's text, captured run or anything copied from published writing, in `SKILL.md` or in `references/`. Print the shape, never the sample: what the sentence does, not the sentence.
- No evals, in any form. No `evals/` folder, no cases, fixtures, good-and-bad pairs or runner, and no mention of the concept inside a skill. A script's checks are proved by its own `--self-test`; a skill folder holds `SKILL.md` plus `references/` and `scripts/`, and nothing else.
