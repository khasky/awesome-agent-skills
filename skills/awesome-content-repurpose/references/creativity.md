# Creativity - what the dial moves, and what it never moves

The interview asks for a number from 1 to 10 (`SKILL.md` Phase 1). This file says what each level permits, what it requires, and what stays fixed at every level. Read it at Phase 1, before the brief.

## 1. The problem the dial exists to solve

A post can satisfy every rule in this skill and still read as machine-made, because the rules decide one shape and the run applies it to whatever came in. Measured on earlier packs: almost every sentence sat between 15 and 35 words, the same sentence-opening stems recurred across unrelated subjects, and nothing was false. The dial decides how much of the shape the run may choose for itself.

The fix for uniform rhythm is not a prescribed rhythm. Mechanical long-short-long alternation is its own fingerprint. Every rhythm rule below sets a **spread** and bans **clustering**, and none prescribes an order.

## 2. The floor no level lifts

The dial governs shape and phrasing. It never governs truth. At level 3 exactly as at level 1:

- every number keeps the condition it was verified under, every peer stays the one Phase 3 locked, every command stays line for line what the source or the official page shows;
- no fabricated first-person experience, no manufactured stakes, no invented specifics, no performed candor, no contrarian stance the source does not support. The run's own acts are never the author's;
- each form's ceiling, the frontmatter contract, the footer byte for byte;
- ASCII punctuation and no semicolons;
- the caveat's content and any safety-bearing sentence: their wording may move at levels 2 and 3, what they oblige the reader to do may not;
- the source promise and the module coverage of `references/fidelity.md`.

When a level's permission and the floor disagree, the floor wins and the report says which sentence it held back.

## 3. The scale and the levels

One ten-step dial, 1 the plainest and 10 the freest. Three points carry names: **Normal is 1, Medium is 5, High is 8**. Every number resolves to a level, and the level decides the rules:

| Number | Level |
| --- | --- |
| 1, 2, 3, 4 | 1 - Normal |
| 5, 6, 7 | 2 - Medium |
| 8, 9, 10 | 3 - High |

A number inside a level changes nothing beyond the level's rules. It is recorded as given, and the report says whether it was picked or typed. There is no level below 1: a setting that only replayed the default shape came out uniform without coming out safer.

| Axis | 1 - Normal | 2 - Medium | 3 - High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Structure** | the shapes in `references/authored-style.md` section 4, in the source's order | the register leads: sections may be merged, reordered or opened on the failure or the answer, where the material reads better that way | composed from the module map; the shapes inform and do not decide |
| **Anchors** | each form writes the fact set in its own words, with every number, condition and named case identical | the same | the same |
| **Rhythm** | floor B (section 4), fragments allowed outside the short form | floor C, fragments common, one self-correction and one hedge in the long form | floor C, one-word sentences allowed outside the short form |
| **Openers, titles, closings** | the moves and tests in `references/authored-style.md` | invented moves allowed when they pass the opener tests | free, with the opener tests and the title rules the only survivors |
| **Register authority** | sets lexicon, emoji baseline, question habit and heading style | also decides structure and how long each form runs under its ceiling | decides everything the floor does not |
| **Frame liberty** | contractions, direct address, one aside in the long form | idiom, hedges, visible self-correction, opinion stated plainly | humor, a running voice, asides anywhere, the author's own vocabulary |
| **Repetition** | the limits in `references/authored-style.md` section 11 | no prose sentence shared by two forms except an anchor | no non-technical 6-word span shared by two forms, and each form carries one concrete detail the others do not |

As format constraints loosen, sameness constraints tighten, because repetition is the tell that survives every other change.

## 4. Rhythm, as spread rather than as pattern

The floors come from published posts measured on the platforms these files land on. A technical write-up of 57 sentences ran a mean of 20.5 words, a spread of 2 to 65, 39% inside the 10-to-20-word band, and a longest run of 4 near-equal sentences. A career essay of 109 sentences ran a mean of 14.7, a spread of 2 to 40, the same 39% in the band, and the same run of 4. Human prose is far more spread than generated prose, and it is not free of repetition either: a run of four near-equal sentences turned up in both.

The floors, measured on the prose of one file after the frontmatter, excluding code, lists and the footer:

```text
floor B (level 1)   longest at least 25 words longer than the shortest
                    at most 60% inside the 10-to-20-word band
                    at least 1 sentence under 7 words per 300 words of body
                    at most 4 consecutive sentences within 3 words of each other

floor C (levels 2-3) longest at least 30 words longer than the shortest
                    at most 50% inside the band
                    at least 1 sentence under 7 words per 200 words of body
                    at most 4 consecutive sentences within 3 words of each other
                    paragraph lengths in the long form span at least 1 to 5 sentences
```

A file under 200 words of prose is measured on the spread and the run only. The short form is measured on the run only: three or four sentences of near-equal length inside 280 characters is most of what makes a short post read as generated.

**The run limit is one-directional.** It forbids monotony and does not ask for alternation. A file whose lengths read long-short-long-short has failed the spirit of the floor while passing its arithmetic: report it and rewrite it.

## 5. Tell density rather than a ban list

A banned-word list produces prose that dodges the list. Count instead, per 200 words of body: a single machine-favored construction is noise, three in one paragraph is a rewrite of that paragraph. What counts as one:

- a nominalization where a verb was available, a transition word that names a relation the sentence order already shows (`additionally`, `moreover`, `furthermore`), a triad where the material has two items or four;
- the `not X, but Y` frame, the `it is not only ... but also` frame, an announced thought (`the key insight is that`), a summary that restates the paragraph above it;
- a stock intensifier (`crucial`, `pivotal`, `robust`, `seamless`, `powerful`), an abstraction standing where a named thing belongs;
- a sentence whose subject is the post itself.

The abstraction standing where the method or the product has a name is the one that arrives at every level, usually while the name sits in the run's own evidence notes. Technical terms that are the only precise word are not tells, and a construction quoted from the source keeps its quotation marks and is exempt.

## 6. What the level does to the audit

```text
P0  floor breach: a fact, a number, a peer, a command, a ceiling, a frontmatter
    key, an invented specific, a missing module. Blocks at every level.
P1  the level's own requirement missed: rhythm floor, repetition threshold,
    register mismatch. Blocks.
P2  polish inside the level's allowance. Reported, fixed where cheap, never blocking.
```

The report carries, per file: sentence count, mean, shortest and longest, the share inside the band, the longest run of near-equal sentences, and the tell count. Count them. A number that was not counted is not reported.

## 7. Anti-patterns of the dial

- Treating a higher level as permission to invent a detail, a result, a stake or an opinion.
- Writing long-short-long to satisfy a spread floor.
- Letting a register loosen the caveat or a safety-bearing line.
- Re-voicing a setup block, a config block or a command at any level.
- Reporting rhythm or tell figures that were never counted.
- Raising the level to rescue a thin source. A source with two facts makes a short long form at every level.
