# minds

Minds replaces your uploaded image with a link-preview card the moment the body carries a URL, and the swap is silent. After typing a body containing `https://fal.live/`, the composer's only media was `cdn.minds.com/api/v2/media/proxy?size=1200&src=https%3A%2F%2Ffal.live%2F` — the target site's 1200×630 OG image — while the `blob:` preview of the campaign picture was gone, even though `input.files.length` still read 1. Publishing there ships someone else's banner as the post's image. Order fixes it: upload the picture first, into the inline composer, without touching the trigger overlay, and only then focus the textarea and type. Verify before submitting by looking for a `blob:` image whose `naturalWidth` matches the source file, and treat any `media/proxy` `img` as the card, not your upload. Where the card has already displaced the image, its own `×` removes it; re-upload afterwards and re-check.

Clicking the trigger overlay opens a second, modal composer, and then two of everything exist. The run ended up with `document.querySelectorAll('input[type=file]').length === 2` — one belonging to the inline composer behind the modal, one to the modal — with `page.$('input[type=file]')` silently taking the wrong one, so uploads landed in a composer that was not the one being submitted. When the DOM shows two file inputs or two Post buttons, the state is unrecoverable by targeting harder: reload, verify the composer is empty and single, and redo it image-first. Nothing is published until Post, so a reset costs nothing.

Submit needs `el.click()` from `page.evaluate`. Coordinate clicks and `handle.click()` both fail here (the latter on actionability), while the untrusted click publishes immediately.

Do not pick the submit by ordinal. Two or more buttons read `Post` and which index is the live one depends on whether you are in the inline composer or the modal `Compose` dialog, so a run that took `btns[0]` in the modal clicked a control outside the panel: the dialog closed, the textarea cleared to length 0 — the documented confirmation of a successful publish — and nothing was created. Absence had to be proven across the profile twice, the feed and the gallery before it was safe to retry. Take the `Post` inside the composer you are actually in (`closest('[role=dialog]')` when the modal is open, the lowest candidate in that panel), and treat the cleared textarea as necessary but not sufficient: only the profile's newest `/newsfeed/<id>` moving proves it. A `Discard changes?` confirm appearing means a click landed on navigation rather than submit — answer `accept: false` to keep the draft and re-target.

On the permalink the body is collapsed and the expander is easy to mis-click. The post renders ~450 characters and a `see more`; clicking every element whose text matches navigated the tab to a *different* post. Match on a leaf node (`e.children.length === 0`) whose text is exactly `see more`, click it by coordinate, then compare the tail — expanded, the post showed its closing line and all three URLs as anchors.

Check: `minds.com/newsfeed/subscriptions` — the rail renders Newsfeed / Boost / Wallet and the `@handle` entry. Compose: the composer sits on the newsfeed as `textarea.m-composerTextarea__message` ("Speak your mind...").

Keystrokes only reach it through an element handle. A coordinate click plus `page.keyboard.type` leaves the value empty and both "Post" buttons disabled, and so does `el.focus()`; `page.$('textarea.m-composerTextarea__message')` then `handle.type(text)` fills it correctly first try. The enabled submit is not in the page-wide button sweep either — walk up from the textarea (about five parents) to the composer container and take the "Post" button inside it.

Read-back: `minds.com/<handle>/` shows the post and the full URL text; the permalink is the `/newsfeed/<numeric-id>` anchor. Never touch Boost, Wallet, Supermind or Minds+ controls — the token layer sits next to the composer and none of it is part of posting.

`.m-composer__triggerOverlay` sits on top of the composer and eats the click on Post. With the body typed and the Post button enabled, `elementFromPoint` at the button's centre returns that overlay, so every trusted click lands on it. Click the overlay first: the composer expands into a modal, and from then on there are two `textarea.m-composerTextarea__message` nodes holding the same text and two Post buttons — the inline one enabled, the modal's one disabled. Take the last enabled Post and click it with `el.click()` from `page.evaluate`; both textareas clear on success.

The permalink page renders the whole feed, so `document.querySelector('.m-activityContent__message')` on it can return a *different* post's body, and the post's own text stays collapsed behind `See more` whose expand control detaches on re-render. Do not reach for Minds' API to get around it. A run navigated the tab to `/api/v1/entities/entity/<guid>` and was served a Cloudflare block page — the account being noticed, on a platform where a ban costs the user everything the account holds. Expand `See more` and read the body visually, or where a body-level assertion cannot be made, say which one and leave it unverified rather than claiming it.
