An empty Draft.js editor reports `innerText.length === 1`, not 0 (the empty block's newline). A submit-confirmation poll that waits for exactly 0 never fires; treat `<= 1` as cleared.

**Tier and the 280 cap.** Read the account's cap from the composer, never from a verified badge: on a non-Premium account a text past 280 turns the counter negative and shows `Upgrade to Premium to write longer posts`. One preflight read a badge as Premium, and the post went out without its link. Measure in Phase 3b with the URL at 23 and each emoji at 2.

**The link outranks the tags and the text (user's rule, 2026-10-01).** When body + link exceed the cap, the link stays whole and the text gives way: shorten it by meaning — tighten or merge a clause, keep every fact and the claim the post makes — until the link fits, then add whole tags from the front of the pool while they still fit, and none where none fit. Never ship an X post without its link to keep a tag or a sentence. The shortened text goes into the Phase 3b batch for approval. Worked case: a 281-unit body + link became 265 by turning "Google compared it with A, B and C on 19 benchmarks. Argon comes first…" into "Against A, B and C on 19 benchmarks, Argon comes first…", which left room for one tag (274). A post that already went out without its link is republished in this shape; the old one is deleted only on the user's per-item request, after the new one is read back.

`Escape` in a filled composer does not close a typeahead; it raises *Discard post?* — answer `Cancel` and the draft stays. A trailing space after the last hashtag is enough to close the typeahead. After the handle click, the profile can show the previous post on top for several seconds: reload once before treating the post as missing, never repost on the first empty read.
