**Tier and the 280 cap.** Read the account's cap from the composer, never from a verified badge: on a non-Premium account a text past 280 turns the counter negative and shows `Upgrade to Premium to write longer posts`. **The link outranks the tags and the text (user's rule, 2026-10-01).** When body + link exceed the cap, the link stays whole and the text gives way: shorten it by meaning — tighten or merge a clause, keep every fact and the claim the post makes — until the link fits, then add whole tags from the front of the pool while they still fit, and none where none fit. Never ship an X post without its link to keep a tag or a sentence. The shortened text goes into the Phase 3b batch for approval. Worked case: a 281-unit body + link became 265 by turning "Google compared it with A, B and C on 19 benchmarks. Argon comes first…" into "Against A, B and C on 19 benchmarks, Argon comes first…", which left room for one tag (274). A post that went out without its link is a defect and takes the remediation ladder in `SKILL.md`: delete it within 1-3 minutes of publishing, then republish in this shape no sooner than 5 minutes after the delete, at the end of the pass. Never leave the defective copy up beside its replacement: two near-identical posts with the same image an hour apart, the first deleted after the second, is what preceded this account's spam label.
